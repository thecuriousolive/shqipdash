From across town to across the Atlantic.

ShqipDash USA is our shopping concierge for the things Albania doesn’t stock. Send us a US product link, we order it for you, and your purchases travel together in one consolidated monthly shipment to Vlorë.

The monthly cycle

  • Order by the 15thSend your links and approve your quote by the 15th of the month to make that month’s shipment.
  • Shipment leaves at month endAll orders are consolidated into one shipment at the end of the month, so international shipping costs are shared by weight.
  • Pickup in VlorëYour items are ready for pickup in Vlorë the following month — we message you the moment they arrive.
How the price works: product price + your share of international shipping + estimated customs/VAT + our 15% service fee ($10 minimum). A 50% deposit is due before we order; the balance is due at pickup. Customs and VAT are estimates until the shipment clears.

You’re updated at four points

  • 1Ordered — your item is purchased and confirmed.
  • 2Arrived — it has reached our US receiving warehouse.
  • 3Dispatched — the consolidated shipment is on its way to Albania.
  • 4Ready for pickup — your item is in Vlorë and ready to collect.

Not accepted without prior approval: perfume, aerosols, medicine, supplements, food or perishables, loose lithium batteries, counterfeit items, weapons, hazardous materials, and very large or heavy items. If you’re unsure about an item, ask us first — we’ll tell you honestly.

Questions, answered straight

Is ShqipDash a delivery company?

No — we’re a concierge and coordination service. You hire us to figure out and arrange what you need: we shop, call, collect and coordinate, using our own local helpers and trusted third-party couriers and stores where that’s the practical option.

Is it only for Americans?

No — ShqipDash is for anyone. We work in English, which makes us a good fit for expats, new residents, visitors and anyone who doesn’t speak Albanian yet.

Can I ask for something that isn’t listed?

Yes. Custom requests are welcome on WhatsApp. We’ll tell you honestly whether it’s something we can do safely, legally and well, and quote it individually.

When do I pay?

It depends on the request. If we’re buying things for you, the product cost and service fee are paid before shopping begins. Pickup-only requests are paid when the request is accepted. For USA shopping, a 50% deposit of the full quote is due before we order, with the balance at pickup. Payment details are sent privately once you approve your quote — nothing is charged on this site.

Can you collect my Temu or courier package?

Often, yes — but it depends on the carrier. Some companies will only release a parcel to the named person or require specific authorization. Send us the tracking details and pickup notice, and we’ll check what the carrier allows before quoting.

Do you mark up product prices?

Never. You pay the store price, and receipts are provided whenever the store issues one. ShqipDash earns only the service fee in your quote.

Can you do same-day?

Often it’s possible, especially for central Vlorë — but it isn’t guaranteed. Message us as early in the day as you can and we’ll tell you honestly what’s realistic.

Do you serve outside Vlorë?

Our focus is Vlorë. Requests in other Albanian cities can sometimes be arranged, with an additional fee — ask us and we’ll see what’s possible.

Can you submit official papers for me?

We can transport documents — pick them up and drop them at an office. What we can’t do is sign documents for you, represent you before authorities, give legal advice, or guarantee that an office will accept a submission. If a filing has to be done by you in person, we’ll tell you before you pay anything.

Can you bring medicine?

We can collect prepaid pharmacy orders and shop for everyday pharmacy items as part of a shopping run. Prescription items follow the pharmacy’s own rules, and we can’t give medical advice or make medical decisions. For USA orders, medicine and supplements are not accepted without prior approval — ask us first.

What if an item is unavailable?

We’ll message you from the store with the closest alternatives and their prices. Nothing is substituted and no extra amount is spent without your approval — and if we can’t complete a request at all, unused product funds and unearned service fees are refunded.
